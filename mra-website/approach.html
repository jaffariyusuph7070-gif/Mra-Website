01. THE SEVEN-STAGE PROCESS

From Listening to Evaluation: Our Consulting Lifecycle

After listening, we embark on a thorough research, development and pre-testing before delivery. Every stage is designed to ensure relevance, precision, and assured quality.

  1. 01

    LISTEN

    We begin every assignment by listening closely to client leadership, operational teams, and community stakeholders to understand exact resource and management needs.

  2. 02

    RESEARCH

    We conduct rigorous field research, desk reviews, baseline surveys, and systems analysis to establish an accurate empirical foundation.

  3. 03

    DEVELOP

    We design custom strategies, organizational systems, value chain interventions, or training modules aligned to the client’s vision and scale.

  4. 04

    PRE-TEST

    Before full deployment, we pre-test our instruments, training curricula, and management frameworks to verify practicality and cultural fit.

  5. 05

    DELIVER

    Solutions are delivered through training, strategy development, one-on-one consultations, staff mentoring, and comprehensive analysis reports.

  6. 06

    MONITOR

    We accompany implementation with structured monitoring to track adoption, address emerging bottlenecks, and safeguard resource sustainability.

  7. 07

    EVALUATE

    We conduct systematic evaluations and endline reviews to measure outcomes and ensure our clients remain an edge above the rest.

02. CORE METHODOLOGY PILLARS

Three Governing Principles of Every Assignment

PRINCIPLE 01 · LISTENING FIRST

Understanding Before Proposing

MRA Management Associates believes in listening as a first step of understanding the needs of any client. We can know only through listening to YOU before we can propose and install effective solutions.

PRINCIPLE 02 · RIGOROUS PREPARATION

Research, Development & Pre-Testing

After listening, we embark on a thorough research, development and pre-testing before delivery. Our solutions may be achieved and delivered through training, strategy development, one on one consultations, mentoring, monitoring, evaluation and analysis reports.

PRINCIPLE 03 · ASSURED QUALITY

Specific Solutions for Specific Problems

The bottom line of our consultancy is: each client and need is different! Hence we provide specific solutions for specific problems and need with precision and assured quality.

03. TAILOR-MADE SOLUTIONS

Adapting to Vision, Size and Business Context

For Community Groups & Grassroots Associations: When we work with community groups, we design trainings and institutional support that help them move from low income to middle or higher income levels.

For Medium & Large Organizations and Private Sector Clients: When we work with medium and large organizations and private sector clients, our trainings and strategic advisory are tailored on how to position them on the top — or how to maintain their leadership position.
