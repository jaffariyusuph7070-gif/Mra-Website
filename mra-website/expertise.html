02. TECHNICAL SPECIALIZATIONS

Core Domains of Consultant Competence

Our multidisciplinary associates are deployed according to the specific technical, regional, and institutional requirements of each assignment.

DOMAIN 01

Agricultural Economics & Value Chain Specialists

Extensive field experience mapping and upgrading commodity value chains in Tanzania — including sesame, dairy, beef and hides, horticulture, onions, garlic, rice, sunflower, edible oils, maize, and mango exports.

DOMAIN 02

Monitoring, Evaluation & Survey Methodologists

Specialists in country programme evaluations, baseline and endline surveys, and empirical sector reviews compliant with Data Protection Act standards as a certified Data Controller.

DOMAIN 03

Organizational Development & Strategy Advisors

Senior executives and management consultants experienced in institutional restructuring, strategic planning, management audits, business plans, and executive staff mentoring.

DOMAIN 04

M4P & Market Systems Practitioners

Active contributors to the Tanzania Markets Policy Action Node (TM-PAN), applying Making Markets Work for the Poor (M4P) and systems analysis to project and intervention designs.

DOMAIN 05

Human Rights, Gender & Socio-Economic Analysts

Specialists in human and socio-economic rights assessments in water user groups, rice schemes, and fresh fruit and vegetable value chains, alongside community group strengthening.

DOMAIN 06

Energy, Oil & Gas Sector & Cross-Border Advisory

Registered with EWURA to work in the oil and gas sector, and connected across Canada, Tanzania, Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, and Bangladesh through shareholding in the Enterprise Development Hub.

03. REQUESTING CONSULTANT PROFILES

How We Assemble Assignment Teams

Because MRA Management Associates does not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach, every consultancy team is purposefully composed around the exact Terms of Reference (ToR), sector, and geographic scope of the client’s assignment.
