About MRA Management Associates

Established in 2005 in Dodoma, Tanzania, MRA Management Associates brings together experienced executives, managers, and specialist consultants to help organizations harness knowledge and resources for sustainable institutional growth.

01. COMPANY BACKGROUND SINCE 2005

Who We Are

MRA Management Associates is a professional consultancy firm founded by experienced Executives, Managers and consultants to help organizations explore and fully utilize the potential in the available knowledge and resources within and those arising from the opportunities that exist in today’s ever-changing business and development world.

As a team of experts, MRA Management Associates understands the importance of sustainability of resources in any organization and therefore works with the management to identify the gaps and offer appropriate research, evaluations, knowledge, skills, organizational development, strategy developments and mentoring.

From our headquarters at Plot 75, Kisasa B Centre Block F in Dodoma, Tanzania, our consultants have supported international development agencies, donor agency projects, national NGOs, community groups, and private-sector enterprises across Tanzania and the wider region.

02. STRATEGIC DIRECTION

Our Vision & Mission

Guided by a clear commitment to institutional excellence, cost-effectiveness, and measurable impact.

VISION STATEMENT

Our Vision

To be the best in effectively harnessing the use of available knowledge and resources for the growth of our clients, business and development partners.

MISSION STATEMENT

Our Mission

To closely understand our clients’ resource and management needs and be able to provide robust, precise and cost-effective solutions at all times with a view of enabling our clients to be an edge above the rest.

04. OUR WORK PHILOSOPHY

We Do Not Believe in One Fix for All — We Believe in Tailor-Made

At MRA Management Associates, we do not believe in one fix for all. We believe that our clients are different in vision, size and even business. For instance, when we work with community groups we design trainings that help them move from low income to middle or higher.

On the other hand, when we work with medium and large organisations and private sector clients, our trainings could be tailored on how to position them on the top or even how to maintain their position. Therefore, we believe in tailor-made!

The bottom line of our consultancy is: each client and need is different — hence we provide specific solutions for specific problems and needs with precision and assured quality.
