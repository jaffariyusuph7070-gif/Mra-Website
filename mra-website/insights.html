KNOWLEDGE & DOCUMENTATION REPOSITORY

Five Thematic Streams of MRA Documentation

As part of our core practice in documentation and publication of reports including case studies, our knowledge outputs are organized into five categories.

01 · RESEARCH

Sector Reviews & Empirical Research Studies

Includes our landmark research assignments such as the Analysis of the Tanzanian Dairy Sector (for ACET), the Review of the Success and Failures of Dairy Development in Tanzania (for ILRI), and regional FFV trade market analyses (for USAID COMPETE).

Dairy Sector · Regional Trade · Market Systems
02 · CASE STUDIES

Value Chain Upgrading & Rights Integration Case Studies

Documentation of field interventions in Human Rights and Gender Integration in the Rice and FFV Value Chains in Tanzania (VECO East Africa) and Human and Socio-Economic Rights Assessment in Water User Groups for Rice Schemes (LIC).

Human Rights · Gender · Water User Groups
03 · PUBLICATIONS

Country Working Papers & Commodity Investment Plans

Authorship and technical publication of working papers and investment frameworks, including the Development of Agri-Hubs Dairy Working Group Country Paper (Agri-ProFocus) and the Maize Commodity Investment Plan for Mbeya (ACT/TAP).

Country Papers · Commodity Plans
04 · PROJECT FINDINGS

Baseline Surveys & End-of-Project Evaluation Findings

Evidence syntheses from the End of Project Evaluation in Dodoma and Morogoro Regions (OXFAM GB), Dairy Value Chain Baseline Survey for Tanga Region (SNV), and Evaluations for Fruit Products and Baseline Surveys (MVIWATA).

Baseline Surveys · Endline Evaluations
05 · COMPANY UPDATES

Institutional Compliance, EWURA Registration & Regional Network

Updates on MRA Management Associates’ Data Controller compliance under the Data Protection Act, EWURA registration for the oil and gas sector, TM-PAN membership, and Enterprise Development Hub representation across 6 countries.

Dodoma Headquarters · Institutional News
