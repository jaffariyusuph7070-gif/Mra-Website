Consulting Services & Practice Areas

MRA Management Associates partners with management teams, development agencies, NGOs, and private enterprises to identify institutional gaps and deliver research, evaluations, strategy development, value chain analysis, and staff mentoring.

01. CORE CONSULTING AREAS

11 Core Consulting Practice Areas

Comprehensive advisory capabilities grounded in empirical field research and institutional systems thinking.

Request a Technical Proposal
CORE AREA 01

Organizational Development

Comprehensive organizational diagnostics, institutional strengthening, governance structuring, and capacity development designed to ensure long-term sustainability of organizational resources.

CORE AREA 02

Business, Systems & Strategy Development

Formulation of multi-year strategic plans, commodity investment plans, business plans for commercial stores and enterprises, and operational management systems.

CORE AREA 03

Research, Reviews & Evaluations

Country programme evaluations, end-of-project evaluations, sector-wide reviews (such as the Tanzanian Dairy Sector reviews for ILRI and ACET), and thematic policy studies.

CORE AREA 04

Baseline & Endline Surveys

Rigorous quantitative and qualitative baseline and endline surveys (including dairy value chain baseline surveys in Tanga and fruit product baseline studies for MVIWATA).

CORE AREA 05

Community Group Formation & Strengthening

Community group formations, developments, and strengthening — equipping grassroots producer associations, water user groups, and chapters to transition from low income to middle or higher income tiers.

CORE AREA 06

Project Design

End-to-end project component design and intervention architecture, including DANIDA Phase IV Business Sector Programme Support (BSPS) edible oil component design and SCF intervention designs.

CORE AREA 07

Proposal Development

Development and refinement of bankable funding and grant proposals, including climate-smart agriculture proposals for Tanzania in partnership with AGRA.

CORE AREA 08

Feasibility Studies

Market, technical, and financial feasibility assessments for new business ventures and regional investments, including healthcare enterprise feasibility studies in Lusaka, Zambia.

CORE AREA 09

Value Chain Analysis

Deep empirical value chain mapping and upgrading strategies across sesame, onions, garlic, beef and hides, dairy, horticulture, rice, fresh fruits and vegetables (FFV), sunflower, and mango exports.

CORE AREA 10

Systems Analysis and M4P

Systems analysis and integration of the Making Markets Work for the Poor (M4P) approach in project designs to address root market system constraints sustainably.

CORE AREA 11

Project Management

Full-cycle project management, technical backstopping, implementation monitoring, and results verification for development and corporate programmes.

02. OTHER CONSULTING AREAS

Specialized Assessments, Facilitation & Institutional Systems

Complementary advisory services supporting rights-based development, gender integration, executive mentoring, and institutional documentation.

01. Community Assessments

Participatory community assessments, rural livelihood diagnostics, and grassroots needs appraisals.

02. Human Rights Assessments

Human rights assessments, studies, and reviews — including human rights integration in rice schemes and fresh fruit & vegetable value chains.

03. Socio-Economic Analysis and Gender

Socio-economic rights assessments and gender mainstreaming across agricultural cooperatives, water user groups, and enterprise programmes.

04. Workshop and Meeting Facilitation

Professional facilitation and moderation of multi-stakeholder workshops, commodity working groups, and strategy meetings.

05. Documentation and Publication

Technical documentation and publication of evaluation reports, country working papers, and institutional knowledge products.

06. Case Studies

In-depth empirical case studies documenting lessons learned, successes, and failures across development interventions.

07. Management Audits

Independent management audits evaluating organizational processes, resource utilization, and operational compliance.

08. Staff Mentoring

Structured mentoring of staff and executive teams to build enduring internal management and technical capacity.

09. Organizational Management Systems Development & Analysis

Design, review, and optimization of internal organizational management systems for NGOs, associations, and private companies.

03. TAILORED DELIVERY MODALITIES

How Our Solutions Are Delivered

After listening to your organization and conducting thorough research, development, and pre-testing, our solutions are achieved and delivered through:

  • Tailor-Made Training Programmes (from community groups moving to middle/higher income up to market-leading private corporations)
  • Strategy Development & Commodity Investment Plans
  • One-on-One Executive Consultations
  • Staff & Leadership Mentoring
  • Ongoing Implementation Monitoring
  • Independent Evaluations & Technical Analysis Reports
