1. Who We Are

MRA Management Associates is a professional consultancy firm founded by experienced Executives, Managers and consultants to help organizations explore and fully utilize the potential in the available knowledge and resources within and those arising from the opportunities that exist in today’s ever-changing business and development world.

As a team of experts, MRA Management Associates understands the importance of sustainability of resources in any organization and therefore works with the management to identify the gaps and offer appropriate research, evaluations, knowledge, skills, organizational development, strategy developments and mentoring.

2. Vision & Mission

Vision: To be the best in effectively harnessing the use of available knowledge and resources for the growth of our clients, business and development partners.

Mission: To closely understand our clients’ resource and management needs and be able to provide robust, precise and cost-effective solutions at all times with a view of enabling our clients to be an edge above the rest.

3. Our Founding Principles & Values

4. Core & Specialized Consulting Areas

Core Consulting Areas:

  • Organizational development including business, Systems and Strategy development
  • Research, Reviews and Evaluations including Baseline and endline surveys
  • Community group formations, developments and strengthening
  • Project designs including proposal developments and feasibility studies
  • Value chain analysis
  • Systems analysis and use of M4P in project designs
  • Project management

Other Consulting Areas:

  • Community assessments, human rights assessments, studies and reviews
  • Socio-economic analysis and gender
  • Facilitation and moderation of workshops and meetings
  • Documentation and publication of reports including case studies
  • Management audits and Mentoring of staff
  • Organisational management systems development and analysis

5. Work Philosophy & Approach

At MRA Management Associates, we do not believe in one fix for all. We believe that our clients are different in vision, size and even business. Therefore, we believe in tailor-made solutions: Listen → Research → Develop → Pre-test → Deliver → Monitor → Evaluate.
